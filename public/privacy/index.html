Privacy Policy

How we handle the information behind your plan.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Your budget is yours.

1. Who operates Salary Save

Salary Save is a personal budgeting and expense tracking product operated independently by Ayush Puri. “Salary Save”, “we”, “our” and “us” in this notice refer to this product and its individual operator, not to a separately registered company.

This notice covers the Salary Save web app at salarysave.in and its onboarding design preview. Our privacy and support contact is salarysave@gmail.com.

About this preview: the onboarding preview simulates Google sign-in. Figures entered in it stay in the current page’s memory and are cleared when that page reloads. They are not saved to your Salary Save account. The hosting platform can still process the technical information needed to deliver the page.

2. Information we handle

Account information
When you sign in to the live app using Google, Firebase Authentication handles your sign-in identifier, email address, display name and any basic profile information Google supplies. Salary Save does not receive your Google password.
Information you enter
Your salary, monthly budget allocations, savings targets, expense and savings entries, amounts, dates, categories, names and optional notes.
Preferences and sync information
Settings such as currency, language, appearance and haptics, together with account identifiers and record revisions needed to save and sync your plan.
Technical information and messages
Our authentication and hosting providers may process IP addresses, browser or device information, session identifiers and request or security logs. If you email us, we receive the email address and message you send.

The current app does not connect to bank accounts, read bank passwords, retrieve bank statements, scan SMS messages or request access to contacts. Do not put account passwords, card numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information in entry notes or support messages.

3. How we use your information

  • Sign you in and associate your plan with your account.
  • Calculate and display your allocations, spending and savings history.
  • Save changes and make your account information available when you use the app on another device.
  • Keep the service working, investigate errors and protect accounts against misuse.
  • Respond to support, privacy and data requests, and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not include advertising or third-party audience analytics SDKs in the current app. Your budget entries are not submitted to a generative AI service by the app’s current features. If a future feature needs a new use of personal data, we will explain that use and obtain any consent required before enabling it.

4. Providers and sharing

We use Google Firebase Authentication for Google sign-in and Cloud Firestore for account budget storage. Hosting and delivery are provided through OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare infrastructure. Some hosted previews also require a separate platform sign-in.

These providers handle information needed for their services under their own terms and privacy arrangements. We may also disclose information where required by law, to respond to a valid legal process, or where reasonably necessary to investigate security incidents or protect users’ rights. We do not make your salary or entries publicly visible.

Provider information: Firebase privacy and security, OpenAI Privacy Policy, and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Provider infrastructure can process information in India or in other countries where it operates. We do not promise that all information remains in India.

5. Security and browser storage

The hosted service uses HTTPS to protect information in transit. Firebase documents encryption in transit and at rest for Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. The app uses signed-in, account-specific access controls for budget records.

This is not end-to-end encryption: the service and its providers can process information to operate the app, provide authorised support or meet legal obligations. No online service can guarantee that every security risk is eliminated.

Your browser can retain authentication/session information and older local app data. Salary Save does not add its own encryption to browser storage. Protect your device and Google account, sign out on shared devices, and clear site data when appropriate. Clearing browser data or signing out does not delete cloud account records.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain account and budget information while it is needed to provide your account, until you remove it or request deletion, subject to any necessary legal or security retention. The current app does not automatically erase inactive accounts after a fixed period.

Deleting an entry in the app removes it from the account’s active budget record when the change successfully syncs. To delete your account and its active budget record, open Profile → Delete account and verify your Google account. For help, email salarysave@gmail.com. A separate request may be needed to remove old data retained only on your own browser.

Provider backups and security records may remain for their applicable retention periods. We will explain any information that must be kept when handling a request rather than promise immediate erasure from every backup.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You can edit your salary, allocations and entries in the app, delete individual entries and sign out. You can also email us to request access, a copy, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent or help with a privacy concern.

Send requests to salarysave@gmail.com, preferably from the email address used for your Google sign-in. We may need to verify that you control the relevant account. Do not send your password or a copy of an identity document unless we specifically explain why another verification method is insufficient.

We will handle requests within the periods required by applicable law. If information is necessary to operate an account and you withdraw permission to use it, some features may stop working or the account may need to close. Your statutory rights and available complaint routes remain unaffected.

Privacy requirements in India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and related Rules, have phased commencement dates. We will apply the obligations and rights that are in force and applicable to the service. This notice is not a claim of regulatory certification.

8. Age, updates and contact

Salary Save is intended for people aged 18 or older. If you believe a child has provided account information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

We may update this notice as the product or law changes. The date above shows the latest revision; material changes will be brought to users’ attention where required. New uses of information will be subject to any required notice and consent.
